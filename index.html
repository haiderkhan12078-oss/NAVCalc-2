Operational transformation

One close. Two operating models.

Research into fund-admin workflows consistently points to the same control chain: complete data, reconciliation, valuation and accrual checks, exception handling, approval, and retained evidence.

01Structured inputs

Positions, cash, pricing and period data arrive into one controlled cycle.

02Reconciliation

System checks populations and routes only real breaks for review.

03Valuation & fees

Configured logic runs consistently instead of being rebuilt every period.

04Validation

Movement, completeness and tolerance controls identify unusual items.

05Human review

People focus on exceptions and evidence rather than repetitive steps.

06Approval & output

Reviewer sign-off and reporting remain explicit and traceable.
